# Hi, I'm Jordan 👋

**Network Architect → Full-Stack Developer & DevOps Automation Engineer.** I build resilient networks and the software that operates them, on-premises and in the cloud.

---

## What I'm known for

I defined a minimal JSON schema and SDK-driven automation for **SD-WAN provisioning** across Meraki, Cisco, and VeloCloud that took deployment from **one hour per site to fifteen minutes for hundreds of sites** — at the click of a button.

That is the shape of most of my work: find the manual process that scales linearly with headcount, and replace it with something that doesn't.

## How I work

I'm a reliability and automation engineer who believes in **proactive over reactive**.

My "Future Lazy Engineering" approach means investing extra effort up front — anticipating edge cases, then delivering the bare-minimum modular code that scales. Doing the work early prevents the cascading tech debt of Band-Aid fixes. A two-hour patch that gets skipped has a habit of becoming a five-to-eight-week project.

By day I design and secure enterprise-scale networks. By night I build React/TypeScript applications, script Python automations, and weave AI into every workflow I can reach.
